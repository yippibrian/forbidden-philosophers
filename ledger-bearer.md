# THE LEDGER-BEARER

## An Anti-Odyssey in Rustic Episodes

### *A Philosophical Telenovela About Return, Repair, and Home*

---

# SECTION 1 — THE DEPARTURE / LA SALIDA 🌾

*(Everyone agrees to go home; no one defines home)*

---

*(DARKNESS.)*

*(A rooster crows.)*

*(A second rooster crows.)*

*(A THIRD rooster crows, but dramatically. Too dramatically.)*

*(Thunder.)*

**NARRATOR (too serious, already exhausted):**
After the systems fell...

after the Algorithm stopped humming...

after the goat ascended into mystery...

after everyone promised, sincerely and incorrectly,
that they had learned...

there remained one problem.

*(Beat.)*

They had to go home.

*(A warm golden light rises. It looks like dawn over a ranch. This is strange, because we were not on a ranch.)*

**NARRATOR:**
But home is not a place.

*(A pause.)*

Home is a reconstruction problem.

*(Another pause.)*

Also, apparently, a ranch.

*(LIGHTS UP.)*

## SCENE 1 — THE FIELD THAT SHOULD NOT BE HERE

*(EL CAMPO QUE NO DEBERÍA EXISTIR)*

*(Morning. A wide rural field. Dry grass. A low stone wall. A broken fence. A suspiciously beautiful barn. Mountains in the distance. Somewhere, a guitar plays one tragic chord and then stops, embarrassed.)*

*(At center stage: THE LEDGER, sitting open on a wooden table.)*

*(ARISTOTLE, PLATO, PYTHAGORAS, DIOGENES, HYPATIA, and SOCRATES stand around it.)*

*(The CHORUS is nowhere obvious.)*

**ARISTOTLE:**
This is impossible.

**PLATO:**
No.

This is symbolic.

**PYTHAGORAS:**
The field has poor proportions.

**DIOGENES:**
The field has dirt.

That’s usually enough.

**SOCRATES:**
Where are we?

*(Silence.)*

**HYPATIA:**
That is the wrong first question.

**SOCRATES:**
Good.

What is the right first question?

**HYPATIA:**
Who has responsibility for the Ledger.

*(Everyone looks at the Ledger.)*

*(The Ledger turns one page by itself.)*

**PLATO:**
It heard you.

**DIOGENES:**
It always hears.

**ARISTOTLE:**
Books do not hear.

*(The Ledger SLAMS shut.)*

*(Beat.)*

**ARISTOTLE:**
Some books may respond to auditory stimulus.

**DIOGENES:**
Coward.

## SCENE 14 — THE SONG OF INCORRECT PURPOSE

## SCENE 1 — THE CART WILL NOT GO / EL CARRO NO SE MUEVE

*(Morning. The same impossible ranch field. The cart stands center stage, harnessed, rose-decorated, emotionally alert.)*

*(ODYSSEUS stands beside it, travel cloak arranged heroically.)*

*(ARISTOTLE has scrolls. PLATO has longing. PYTHAGORAS has string. DIOGENES has bread. HYPATIA has patience, but not much.)*

*(The CHORUS is preparing for departure as if this were a cattle drive, a wedding, and a funeral at the same time.)*

**CHORUS MEMBER #1:**
Rope?

**CHORUS MEMBER #2:**
Rope.

**CHORUS MEMBER #3:**

**CHORUS MEMBER #1:**
Water.

**CHORUS LEADER:**

*(They nod. This is apparently true.)*

**ODYSSEUS:**

*(The cart does nothing.)*

## SCENE 2 — ARISTOTLE ALMOST SOLVES SOMETHING / ARISTÓTELES CASI RESUELVE ALGO

*(The cart has moved perhaps ten feet. Everyone treats this like a major logistical victory.)*

*(ARISTOTLE climbs onto a rock and unrolls a scroll.)*

**ARISTOTLE:**
I have identified the problem.

**DIOGENES:**
A terrible beginning.

**ARISTOTLE:**
No.

A necessary one.

We have been asking the wrong operational question.

The question is not:

“How do we reach Ithaca?”

*(He writes.)*

The correct sequence is:

What is the destination?

What is the load?

What constraints govern passage?

What obligations remain active?

What harms are reversible?

What losses must be carried rather than corrected?

*(Silence.)*

**HYPATIA:**
Good.

## SCENE 2 — CIRCE ENTERS, HAVING ALREADY UNDERSTOOD EVERYONE / CIRCE ENTRA, YA HABIENDO ENTENDIDO A TODOS

**CIRCE:**
Also your shape.

The repeated gesture.

**CIRCE:**

For others, less so.

No.

I remove ambiguity.

**HYPATIA:**
That is often a curse.

**CIRCE:**
Only if ambiguity was doing work.

*(Beat.)*

**DIOGENES:**
I hate how good that was.

**CIRCE:**
Thank you.

## SCENE 15 — THE FIRST ENTRY WRITTEN BY HAND / LA PRIMERA ENTRADA ESCRITA A MANO

*(The courtyard table.)*

*(Both Ledgers lie open: the journey Ledger and the household Ledger.)*

*(For the first time, neither writes by itself.)*

*(A pen rests between them.)*

**PLATO:**
Has it stopped?

**HYPATIA:**
No.

It is waiting.

**ARISTOTLE:**
For what?

**PENELOPE:**
For us.

*(She takes the pen.)*

*(She writes.)*

RETURN NOT RESTORATION.
READING BEGUN.
TERMS PENDING.

*(She passes the pen to Telemachus.)*

*(He hesitates.)*

*(Writes.)*

FATHER PRESENT.
NOT YET KNOWN.

*(He passes the pen to Servant #1.)*

*(They write.)*

DISMISSED SISTER STILL ABSENT.
ENTRY REOPENED.

*(The pen passes.)*

**SUITOR-ADMINISTRATOR #1 writes:**

> **STOREHOUSE DEBT ACKNOWLEDGED.**

**SUITOR-ADMINISTRATOR #3 writes:**

> **EXCESS FOOD OWED.**

**DIOGENES:**
That is the funniest restitution.

**PENELOPE:**
Do not make it easier.

**DIOGENES:**
Right.

*(The pen passes to Aristotle.)*

ARISTOTLE writes:

> **SORT ACTIONS, NOT PERSONS.**

*(He passes it to Pythagoras.)*

PYTHAGORAS writes:

> **NO REPAIR WITHOUT REST INTERVALS.**

*(To Plato.)*

PLATO writes:

> **MOURN WITHOUT MAKING BEAUTY ACQUIT.**

*(To Socrates.)*

SOCRATES writes:

> **QUESTIONS MUST NOT DELAY REPAIR.**

*(To Hypatia.)*

HYPATIA writes:

> **REPAIR UNDER CHOSEN SCOPE. REVISE TERMS.**

*(To the Chorus.)*

CHORUS LEADER writes:

> **CONTEXT LABOR RECOGNIZED.**

**CHORUS MEMBER #1 writes:**

> **BRIDGE WORK CONTINUES TOMORROW.**

**CHORUS MEMBER #2 writes:**

> **BEAN LOSSES TO BE MEMORIALIZED APPROPRIATELY.**

**CHORUS MEMBER #3 writes:**

> **GOAT FED BEFORE INTERPRETATION.**

*(The goat bleats.)*

**DIOGENES:**
Do not interpret it.

*(Everyone freezes.)*

*(They almost do.)*

*(They do not.)*

*(The goat is fed.)*

*(The pen comes to Diogenes.)*

**DIOGENES:**
No.

**HYPATIA:**
Write.

**DIOGENES:**
I refuse symbolic closure.

**PENELOPE:**
Then write badly.

*(Beat.)*

**DIOGENES:**
Acceptable.

*(He writes one word.)*

FISH.

*(Everyone stares.)*

**ARISTOTLE:**
Why?

**DIOGENES:**
It mattered.

**PLATO:**
How?

**DIOGENES:**
No.

*(Beat.)*

Just record it.

*(The Ledger accepts it.)*

*(Finally, the pen comes to Odysseus.)*

*(He does not write immediately.)*

**ODYSSEUS:**
I wanted to write “I have returned.”

**PENELOPE:**
I know.

**ODYSSEUS:**
I won’t.

*(He writes.)*

I AM HERE TO READ, WORK, AND BE ANSWERED.
RETURN BEGUN.

*(The pen rests.)*

*(No thunder.)*

*(No applause.)*

*(No song.)*

*(Then the Chorus quietly begins—not a performance. A work rhythm.)*

**CHORUS:**
> Open book and open hand,
> No one owns alone the land,
> Home is not the old door wide,
> Home is work on either side.

> Feed the goat and mend the beam,
> Wake from story, not from dream,
> Write the loss and share the load,
> Return begins beside the road.

*(No one claps.)*

*(Some join the work.)*

FINAL SCENE — NO ENDING, BUT A DIFFERENT MACHINE / SIN FINAL, PERO OTRA MÁQUINA

*(Morning.)*

*(The bridge is still temporary.)*

*(The household is still tense.)*

*(Penelope and Odysseus have not resolved their marriage.)*

*(Telemachus still does not know what to do with his father.)*

*(The servants still have entries.)*

*(The suitors are not absolved.)*

*(The beans are being counted.)*

*(The goat is eating something it should not.)*

**ARISTOTLE:**
The goat is eating the scope notes.

**DIOGENES:**
Good.

They were too long.

**ARISTOTLE:**
They were one page.

**DIOGENES:**
Too long for a goat.

**PLATO:**
I am tempted to say the goat represents the refusal of excessive abstraction.

**HYPATIA:**
And?

**PLATO:**
And I will feed it instead.

*(He does.)*

**SOCRATES:**
What is home?

*(Everyone looks at him.)*

**SOCRATES:**
Too soon?

**PENELOPE:**
Ask while carrying wood.

*(Socrates picks up wood.)*

**SOCRATES:**
What is home while carrying wood?

**DIOGENES:**
Better.

**ODYSSEUS:**
A place where the question has consequences.

*(Silence.)*

**PENELOPE:**
Maybe.

*(Beat.)*

Bring that beam.

*(He does.)*

*(The Narrator steps forward, but hesitates.)*

**NARRATOR:**
And so—

*(He stops.)*

No.

Not “and so.”

*(He looks at the table. The Ledgers. The bridge. The people working.)*

**NARRATOR:**
The story did not end.

The bow did not decide.

The hero did not restore the house.

The house did not forgive by recognizing him.

The record did not vanish.

The losses did not become beautiful enough to stop hurting.

*(Beat.)*

But the record was no longer writing alone.

*(The Chorus continues working.)*

**NARRATOR:**
That was new.

*(The goat bleats.)*

*(Everyone pauses.)*

*(No one interprets it.)*

*(They feed the goat.)*

**DIOGENES:**
Finally.

*(The Ledger, by hand, receives one last entry from no single author. Several hands write it together.)*

> **HOME NOT RESTORED.**
> **HOME RENEGOTIATED UNDER RECORD.**
**RETURN ACHIEVED:**
NO.
**RETURN BEGUN:**
YES.

*(The bridge holds as one person crosses.)*

*(Then another.)*

*(Then another.)*

*(BLACKOUT.)*
